Every flavor in TTN List is built from the same three levels. Learning them
once means you understand Shopping, Chores, and Projects all at the same time.

## The three levels

1. **Items** are the leaves — a single thing to buy, a chore to do, or a step
   to take. They live in your library and can be reused everywhere.
2. **Groups** bundle items under one name — a recipe, a cleaning routine, a
   repeatable process. Add the bundle to a list and all its members come along.
3. **Lists** are saved, ordered selections of items and groups. A list is what
   you actually run as a checklist.

## What each flavor calls them

| Level | Shopping | Chores | Projects |
| ----- | -------- | ------ | -------- |
| Item | Item | Chore | Step |
| Group | Group | Routine | Process |
| List | Shopping List | Chore List | Project List |

## What you'll see

On any flavor's home screen you'll find cards for **Lists**, **Items**, and
**Groups** (plus **History**). A list can mix loose items with whole groups;
when you run it, each group expands into its members.
