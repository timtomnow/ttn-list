Photos help you recognize things at a glance and record how a run went. An
item or group can carry a single thumbnail; a completed run can hold many —
handy for haul shots, before/afters, or receipts.

## Add a photo to an item or group

1. Open an item or group for editing.
2. Under **Photo**, choose to take a new photo or pick one from your gallery.
3. Save. The photo shows as a thumbnail wherever that item or group appears.

## Add photos to a run

1. Run a list and tap **Wrap up** when you finish.
2. Under **Photos**, add one or more shots.
3. Save the run.

## What you'll see

Photos are stored on your device (inside the browser), not on a server. They
travel with your data when you export a backup and come back on import.
