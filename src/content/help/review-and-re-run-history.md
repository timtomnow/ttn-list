Every run you wrap up is saved as a session. History lets you revisit what you
did — including photos and notes — and kick off the same list again.

## Steps

1. Open a flavor and tap the **History** card.
2. Tap a session to open its details.
3. Review the checked state, any **Notes**, and attached photos.
4. To run it again, tap **Re-shop this list** (**Re-run** in Chores and
   Projects).

## What you'll see

Re-running starts a fresh run from the list's *current* contents — not the old
snapshot — so any edits since then are included. If the underlying list has
been deleted, the historical record stays but re-running is unavailable. You
can also delete a session from its detail screen.
