If you step away from a run partway through — close the tab, switch apps, or
tap back to your lists — your progress is saved automatically. You can return
later and continue, or wipe the progress and start fresh.

## Resume where you left off

1. Open the **Lists** card for the flavor.
2. Find the list — its row shows a **Continue** badge with your progress (for
   example, *Continue (3/8)*) instead of the play button.
3. Tap the row to pick up exactly where you left off.

## Start over instead

1. Open the in-progress run.
2. Tap the **Restart** icon (next to **Done**).
3. The run discards your saved progress and re-builds from the list's current
   contents.

## What you'll see

A resumed run keeps every checkbox you'd already ticked. A restarted run comes
back empty and reflects any edits you've made to the list since you last ran
it.
