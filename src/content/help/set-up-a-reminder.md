TTN List doesn't store schedules — instead it generates a calendar file (.ics)
you import into your phone or computer's calendar. The reminder can deep-link
straight back to a specific list.

## Steps

1. Tap **Reminders** in the bottom navigation.
2. Enter an **Event title** and an optional **Message**.
3. Under **Link opens to**, choose the homepage or a specific list so the
   reminder jumps you there.
4. Set the **First occurrence** date and **Time**.
5. Choose how it **Repeats** — One time, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. For Weekly,
   pick the days under **On these days**, and set when it **Ends**.
6. Pick when the **Notification fires** (for example, *15 min before*).
7. Tap **Download calendar file** and open it to add it to your calendar.

## What you'll see

Your calendar app imports the event with its recurrence rule. Tapping the
calendar notification opens TTN List to the list you linked. To change a
reminder later, edit it in your calendar or generate a new file here.
