TTN List is a local-first app for the kind of work you repeat: shopping trips,
chore routines, and multi-step projects. Everything you create lives in your
browser — no account, no server, and it keeps working offline.

## Before you start

- Nothing to set up. Open the app and pick a flavor from the bottom bar:
  **Shopping**, **Chores**, or **Projects**.

## Steps

1. Tap **Shopping**, **Chores**, or **Projects** in the bottom navigation.
2. Build up a **library** of reusable things — items to buy, chores to do, or
   steps to follow.
3. Optionally bundle related ones together (a recipe, a cleaning routine, a
   repeatable process).
4. Assemble a **saved list**, then run it as a checklist when it's time to do
   the work.

## What you'll see

Each flavor opens to the same four cards — **Lists**, **Items**, **Groups**,
and **History** — so once you learn one, you know all three. Your data appears
instantly as you add it and stays on this device.
