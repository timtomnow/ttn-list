Your library holds the reusable pieces for a flavor — a grocery item, a chore,
or a project step. Add something once and you can use it in any number of
lists.

## Steps

1. Open a flavor (**Shopping**, **Chores**, or **Projects**) from the bottom
   bar.
2. Tap the **Items** card (called **Chores** or **Steps** in the other
   flavors).
3. Tap **Add an item** (or **Add one manually** on an empty library).
4. Fill in the **Name** — for example, *Lettuce*.
5. Optionally add **Notes**, one or more **Tags**, and a **Photo**.
6. Tap **Add** to save it to your library.

## What you'll see

The new item appears in the library list right away. You can search by name or
tag, filter by tapping a tag, and reorder items by dragging. Items you add here
are what you'll pick from when building a list.
