You can match the app to your device's light or dark setting and tune how run
mode reads while you're working.

## Steps

1. Tap **Settings** in the bottom navigation.
2. Under **Appearance → Theme**, choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**.
   System follows your device setting.
3. Under **Run view density**, choose **Clean** or **Condensed**.
4. Under **Run view text size**, choose **Small**, **Default**, or **Large**.

## What you'll see

The theme changes immediately across the whole app. The density and text-size
settings affect only run mode — **Shop it**, **Chore it**, and **Run it** — so
you can make the checklist easier to read at a glance.
