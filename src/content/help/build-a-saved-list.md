A list is a saved, ordered selection of items and groups for one run — a
week's groceries, a Saturday chore set, or the steps of a project. It's the
thing you actually check off when it's time to work.

## Before you start

- Add the items (and any groups) you want to include to your library first.

## Steps

1. Open a flavor and tap the **Lists** card.
2. Tap **New list** and give it a **Name** — for example, *Weekly groceries*.
3. Under **Entries**, tap **Item** to add loose items, or **Group** to add a
   whole bundle.
4. In Shopping, set the **quantity** for each entry as needed.
5. To add things just for this list without saving them to your library, type
   them into **Temporary items** (e.g. *celery, remember bread, dish soap*) and
   tap **Add**.
6. Drag entries to put them in the order you want.
7. Tap **Create list** (or **Save changes** when editing).

## What you'll see

The list is saved with its entries in order. From the Lists screen you can run
it, edit it, or drag it to reorder it among your other lists. The order you set
here carries straight into run mode.
