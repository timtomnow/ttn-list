When you're assembling a project list and realise a step doesn't exist yet, you
don't have to stop and go to the library. You can create the step — with a link
and tags — right inside the **Add steps** picker, and it's selected for the list
straight away.

## Before you start

- This is a **Projects** feature. Open **Projects** from the bottom bar.

## Steps

1. Tap the **Lists** card, then **New list** (or open a list and edit it).
2. Under **Entries**, tap **Step** to open the **Add steps** picker.
3. Start typing the step's name in **Search steps or tags**. Existing steps are
   matched by name *and* by tag as you type.
4. If nothing matches, tap **Create new step "…"** (the button shows what you
   typed). On an empty library, tap **Create a new step**.
5. Confirm the **New step name**, optionally add a **Link** and **Tags**, then
   tap **Create & add**.
6. Pick any other steps you want, then tap **Add** to drop them into the list.

## What you'll see

The new step is saved to your Projects library and is automatically ticked in
the picker, so tapping **Add** includes it in the list. Searching by a tag
surfaces matching steps with their tag shown, making it easy to find related
steps later.
