A group bundles several items under one name — a recipe like *Taco Tuesday*, a
weekly cleaning routine, or a repeatable build process. Add the group to a list
and every member comes with it. (Groups are called **Routines** in Chores and
**Processes** in Projects.)

## Before you start

- Add the items you want to bundle to your library first. You can also create
  new items while building the group.

## Steps

1. Open a flavor and tap the **Groups** card (**Routines** or **Processes** in
   the other flavors).
2. Start a new group and give it a **Name** — for example, *Taco Tuesday*.
3. Optionally add **Notes** and a **Photo**.
4. Under **Members**, tap **Add items** and pick the items to include.
5. In Shopping, set each member's default quantity if it differs from 1.
6. Tap **Create group** (or **Save changes** when editing).

## What you'll see

The group shows its member count and appears in the Groups list. When you add
it to a saved list, all its members are pulled in together — and in Shopping,
each member's default quantity is multiplied by the group's quantity on the
list.
